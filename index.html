A Little about Our salon

At Pet’Ceros Grooming! your pet will be styled with a professional stylist who will take the time to connect with them. We commit our efforts to put together your pet’s spa day a relaxing encounter. Our habitat is cage-free , therefore all spa sessions are set by appointment.

We use natural shampoos and conditioners that are custom to each individual pet’s skin and coat type.
